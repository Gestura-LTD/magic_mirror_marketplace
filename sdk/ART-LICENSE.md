## Your own package

Each package says its licences in `manifest.json`:

- `"license"`: the code. One of `MIT` (recommended), `Apache-2.0`, `BSD-2-Clause`, `BSD-3-Clause`.
- `"artLicense"`: the art. `CC-BY-4.0` (recommended) or `CC0-1.0`.

These licences let the MagicMirror_MusicLearningCompanion contributors ship, fix and, if a game is strong enough, build it into the
mirror itself, always with your credit. Use only art you made yourself or art whose licence allows
this; say where it came from in your package's README.
